knotes is the phone side of Nibras PIM, a personal information manager built around plain files. Everything works on the phone without a server; with one, your notes and records sync both ways.

<b>Notes</b>
• Quick capture: type and send, no dialogs. Each note is a plain .md file in a folder you choose.
• Type, priority and category are encoded in the file name, so any tool can read them.
• Arabic and right-to-left text are handled properly. The interface is in English, Arabic and French.
• Collect screenshots, photos and recordings into a note, with a few lines describing the set.

<b>Media player</b>
• Built for long recordings: lectures, audiobooks, voice memos. Remembers where you stopped in every file.
• Bookmarks in plain .tmk files beside the media, shared with the desktop player.
• Subtitles and transcripts, playback speed, volume boost, sleep timer.
• A Videos page with thumbnails; enlarge the video while keeping the controls.

<b>Prayer times</b>
• Computed on the phone for your location, with the Hijri date.
• Athan on time from any audio file you pick, at its own volume level; optional silence after it.
• Home-screen widget with a countdown, and an elapsed timer after each time.

<b>Also</b>
• Alarms, calendar, photo gallery with annotation, flash cards, and home-screen widgets.
• Optional sync with your own Nibras server.

knotes asks for all-files access because notes, media and bookmarks are ordinary files in your folders — that is what lets other apps and computers read them. No ads, no tracking, no accounts with anyone but your own server.
