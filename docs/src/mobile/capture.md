# Quick capture and sharing

Besides typing on the Notes page, there are three faster ways to get something into knotes.

## Share text from another app

In any app that can share text (a browser, a messaging app, a reader):

1. Select or choose the text and tap **Share**.
2. Pick **knotes** from the share sheet.

knotes opens on the Notes page with the text already in the note box. The type, priority and category chips keep the values you used last, so check them, then tap the send arrow to save. Nothing is saved until you do, so you can edit the text first.

!!! note
    knotes accepts shared **text**. To bring in pictures, screenshots or recordings, use [Collect files](collect.md).

## Open audio and video with knotes

In a file manager or any app that offers **Open with**, choose **knotes** for an audio or video file. It opens straight into the [player](player.md), with bookmarks and resume working as usual.

## Add a task or journal entry from the home screen

Two [widgets](widgets.md) have an add bar at the top:

| Widget | Add bar | Saves as |
|---|---|---|
| **Today's tasks** | **+  Add a task…** | A note of type `T` at your **Top priority** |
| **Today's journal** | **+  Write today's journal…** | A note of type `J` at your **Default priority** |

Tap the bar and a one-line input appears just above the keyboard, labelled **Task · p4** or **Journal · today**, with **What needs doing?** or **What happened?** as the prompt. Type and press the keyboard's **Done** key. There is no Add button. The note is written to your notes folder and the widget updates at once.

Typed text is never thrown away:

- Tapping outside the bar closes it only while it's empty. With text in it, the tap is refused and the bar says **Kept. Press Back to leave — what you typed is saved as a draft.**
- If you press Back or Home, what you typed is kept as a draft and comes back the next time you tap the add bar.

!!! tip
    These entries are ordinary notes with an ordinary file name, so they appear on the Notes page too, and on your computer if your notes folder is synced.
