# Quick capture and sharing

Besides typing in the [quick-add bar](notes.md#quick-add) on the Notes page, there are three more ways to get something into Nibras Mobile without opening it first.

## Share text from another app

In any app that can share text (a browser, a messaging app, a reader):

1. Select or choose the text and tap **Share**.
2. Pick **Nibras Mobile** from the share sheet.

The app opens on the [Add note](notes.md#the-full-form-add-note) page with the text already in the box. The type, priority and category chips keep the values you used last, so check them, then tap the send arrow to save. Nothing is saved until you do, so you can edit the text first.

!!! note
    The app accepts shared **text**. To bring in pictures, screenshots or recordings, use [Collect files](collect.md).

## Open audio and video with Nibras Mobile

In a file manager or any app that offers **Open with**, choose **Nibras Mobile** for an audio or video file. It opens straight into the [player](player.md), with bookmarks and resume working as usual. When the file manager doesn't say where the file is, the app finds it on the phone by name and size, so its bookmarks still work; failing that, it plays a temporary copy.

## Add a task or journal entry from the home screen

Two [widgets](widgets.md) have an add bar at the top:

| Widget | Add bar | Saves as |
|---|---|---|
| **Today's tasks** | **+  Add a task…** | A note of type `T` at your **Top priority** |
| **Today's journal** | **+  Write today's journal…** | A note of type `J` at your **Default priority** |

Tap the bar and a one-line input appears just above the keyboard, labelled **Task · p4** or **Journal · today**, with **What needs doing?** or **What happened?** as the prompt. Type and press the keyboard's **Done** key. There is no Add button. The note is written to your notes folder and the widget updates at once (a new task also shows on the [Tasks board widget](widgets.md)).

Typed text is never thrown away:

- Tapping outside the bar closes it only while it's empty. With text in it, the tap is refused and the bar says **Kept. Press Back to leave — what you typed is saved as a draft.**
- If you press Back or Home, what you typed is kept as a draft and comes back the next time you tap the add bar.

!!! tip
    These entries are ordinary notes with an ordinary file name, so they appear on the Notes page too, and on your computer if your notes folder is synced.
